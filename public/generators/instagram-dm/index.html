Fake Instagram DM Generator

Write an Instagram direct-message thread and Mimic draws it in Instagram’s chat, verified badge included if you want one.

Make an Instagram DM

What you can put in

  • One-on-one chats and group chats
  • Reactions, replies and unsent messages
  • Verified badges
  • Photos, stickers, voice messages, links and calls
  • Typing bubbles, and banners dropping in from the top

What you get

  • Screenshots at 1170 × 2532: one screen, or every screen of a long conversation in a zip.
  • One tall image of the whole thing.
  • An MP4 video (1080 × 2338, 30 fps): the conversation plays out with typing, sounds and notifications.
  • The .mimic project file, to keep editing later or in another browser.

How it works

  1. Open Mimic with Instagram picked. It starts from an example you can change.
  2. Type the messages, or paste a whole conversation. Pick who says what, their names and pictures, and the times.
  3. Export screenshots, a long image or a video, and drop it into your edit.

Questions

Is Mimic free?
Yes. No sign-up and no watermark.
Are my conversations uploaded?
No. Everything happens in your browser; projects and pictures stay on your device.
Can I make a video, not just a screenshot?
Yes. Mimic exports an MP4 that plays the scene out, ready for your editor.
Is this the real Instagram?
No. Mimic isn’t affiliated with Instagram: it draws a look-alike for videos, stories and mockups. Please don’t use it to pass off a conversation as real, or to put words in real people’s mouths.
