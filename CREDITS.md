## Default avatars

`public/avatars/` holds 30 animal pictures used until someone sets a photo. They come from OMO's own
PopCafe sticker set (low-poly animals and hand-drawn birds), cropped to head-and-shoulders on a pale
background, 256 × 256 JPEG.

**Status: license not confirmed yet.** Confirm these pictures may be used in another app and appear in
videos people publish before making this repository public; otherwise replace them (same file names).
